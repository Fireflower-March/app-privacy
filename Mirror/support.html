Mirror Support

Need help with Mirror or found a bug? Contact the developer, Xuan Liu, at liuxuan0011@qq.com.

Please include your device model, operating system version, Mirror version, and a brief description of the issue. You do not need to send photos, camera images, or other private information.

How do I use Mirror?

Open Mirror and allow camera access when prompted. Mirror shows a live camera preview at 1080p and 60 frames per second so you can use your device as a mirror. Actual preview performance can vary with your device and system conditions. An internet connection is not required.

What if I denied camera access?

Open your device's system settings, find Mirror's permissions or the camera privacy settings, and enable camera access for Mirror. Then reopen the app. Camera access is needed to display the preview.

Why is the preview black or unavailable?

Check that the camera lens is not covered and that Mirror has camera permission. Close any other app that may be using the camera, then close and reopen Mirror. If the issue continues, restart your device and check for updates to Mirror and your operating system.

Does Mirror store or upload camera images?

No. Mirror only displays the live camera image. It does not perform image recognition, analysis, or additional image processing, and it does not record, save, or upload camera images. It does not require an account, collect personal data, or use advertising or tracking services.

Why doesn't Mirror need other permissions?

Mirror only needs the camera to display the live preview. It does not request microphone, photo library, location, or contacts access.
