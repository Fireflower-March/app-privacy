Mirror Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 28, 2026

English 简体中文 日本語 한국어 Français

Mirror simply displays a live camera preview at 1080p and 60 frames per second. The camera preview does not require an internet connection. Mirror does not analyze, record, save, upload, or collect camera images.

1. Scope and developer

Mirror (the “app”) is developed by Xuan Liu (“we” or “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information. Apple services, this website’s hosting provider, and any communication service you choose to use have their own privacy policies.

2. Camera access and live preview

Mirror requests camera permission solely to show the live camera preview as a mirror. Camera images are used only for live display on your device. Mirror does not analyze or recognize image content or apply additional image processing. Images are not saved as photos or videos, sent to us, or transmitted to a remote service. We cannot view or retrieve your camera feed.

You may decline camera access or revoke it at any time in your device’s system settings. Without camera permission, Mirror cannot display the preview. Camera permission does not grant microphone or photo library access. Mirror does not request access to your microphone, photo library, location, or contacts.

3. Information we do not collect

Mirror does not collect, upload, or share personal information through the app. We do not receive camera images, audio, photos, device or advertising identifiers, location, usage events, analytics, or crash reports through the app.

The app does not require an account, connect to developer-operated servers, or include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or other data collection SDKs. We do not sell or rent app data, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

4. Storage and deletion

Mirror does not keep a photo or video history or retain camera frames after they are needed for the live preview. There is no server-side camera content or app account to delete. Your operating system manages the camera authorization setting; you can change it in system settings.

5. Apple services and this website

Apple independently operates the operating system, App Store, and optional system services. Apple may process information under its own privacy policy and your settings. These services are separate from Mirror’s local camera preview.

Opening this online policy or the support website requires an internet connection. GitHub Pages, which hosts these pages, may process web request information such as your IP address and browser information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages contain no analytics, advertising, remote fonts, third-party scripts, or cookies set by us.

6. Information you send for support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message. We use it only to respond and resolve your request, and retain it only as long as needed for those purposes or as required by applicable law. You can request deletion using the same email address, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. Your email provider may also process your message under its own policy. Please do not send private camera images or other sensitive content.

7. Children’s privacy

The app’s no-collection practices apply to all users, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us by email, contact us so we can address it and delete it as appropriate.

8. Changes to this policy

We will publish changes on this page and update the date above. If the app’s features or data practices change, we will update this policy and relevant App Store disclosures and obtain any permission or consent required before the new processing begins.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent us, contact Xuan Liu at liuxuan0011@qq.com. For help using the app, visit Mirror Support.
