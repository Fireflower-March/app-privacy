Please include your device model, operating system version, ChatBlur version, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send private screenshots or other sensitive information; use a fictional example or redact the content first.

How do I redact a screenshot?

Open ChatBlur and choose Upload Image. Select one image and wait for the automatic scan. Use Brush to cover anything that was missed and Erase to remove unwanted masks. Tap Done after making corrections, review the result, and save a separate copy.

Does Upload send my screenshot to a server?

No. Upload imports an image into the editor. Image analysis and editing run on your device, and ChatBlur does not upload the image to developer-operated servers. Opening this support website or the online privacy policy requires an internet connection.

Why is some information still visible?

Automatic detection can miss details. Check the relevant switches in Settings and use Brush to cover anything else you want to hide. Always review the final image before sharing it.

Why can't I save to Photos?

On iPhone and iPad, allow ChatBlur to add photos when the system requests permission. If access was previously denied, review ChatBlur's Photos permission in system Settings, then try saving again. On Mac, choose a destination using the save panel to export a PNG file.

Does editing change the original image?

ChatBlur saves a separate edited copy. Your original screenshot remains in its original location. Manage originals, exported copies, backups, and recently deleted items using Photos, Files, Finder, or the relevant storage service.

Are my settings remembered?

Masking style, color, and protection switches are saved locally for the next launch. The main app and share extension currently keep their own preferences.

Can I start from another app?

Yes. Share one screenshot or image to ChatBlur using the system share sheet. Edit the image and share the resulting copy to your chosen destination.
