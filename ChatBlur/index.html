ChatBlur Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 18, 2026

English 简体中文 日本語 한국어 Français

ChatBlur is an entirely offline utility app. It does not connect to the internet, collect data from your use of the app, or include third-party data collection SDKs. Content you process in the app stays on your device unless you choose to send or save it elsewhere using other apps or system services.

1. Scope and developer

This policy explains how ChatBlur (the “app”) handles information. ChatBlur is developed by Xuan Liu (“we” or “us”). This policy covers the app; external websites, Apple services, and any services you choose to use have their own privacy policies.

2. Information we do not collect

ChatBlur does not collect, upload, or transmit your personal information or the content you process. We do not receive your photos, screenshots, text, files, or processing results through the app. We do not collect device or advertising identifiers, location, usage events, analytics, or crash reports through the app.

The app does not connect to developer-operated servers and does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or other data collection SDKs. We do not sell, rent, share, or use app data for advertising, profiling, or tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

3. On-device processing and permissions

ChatBlur lets you redact chat screenshots with one tap. You can import screenshots from your photo library or choose ChatBlur in the Photos share sheet to send selected images to the app. The images and any information processed to apply the redaction are handled entirely on your device and are not sent to us or to a remote processing service.

ChatBlur requests photo library permission to access images for editing and, when you choose to save a result to Photos, to save the edited image. iOS manages the authorization and available access levels. You can decline access, limit it to selected photos where supported, or change it later in iOS Settings. Features that require the denied access may be unavailable. Importing an item through a system picker or the share sheet does not by itself grant unrestricted access to your entire library.

Importing a chat screenshot does not give ChatBlur access to your messaging accounts or the live conversations in another app. We do not receive the imported image, its contents, or the edited result.

4. Local storage, retention, and deletion

Any app settings, working files, or other data that the app stores are kept locally in its iOS app container. We do not retain copies on a server and cannot retrieve, restore, or remotely delete your local content.

To remove data stored in the app’s container, use iOS’s Delete App option to remove ChatBlur. Offloading the app is different: iOS may retain its documents and data. Deleting the app does not delete original items or exported copies stored in Photos, Files, other apps, or backups; manage and delete those separately, including any Recently Deleted folders where applicable.

Depending on your device settings, iOS or your chosen storage provider may include local app data or saved items in backups or synchronization, such as iCloud Backup or iCloud Photos. ChatBlur does not operate a cloud service or initiate its own cloud uploads. Manage backups and synchronized copies through the relevant system or service settings.

5. Exporting and sharing

You can export the redacted result and, when you choose to save it to Photos, ChatBlur uses the relevant photo library authorization to save that copy. You can also import selected screenshots by sharing them from Photos to ChatBlur. This handoff makes the selected images available for local processing; it does not upload them to the developer.

If you send an exported image to another app or service, that destination may transmit, synchronize, or otherwise process it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that content. Review the final image before sharing: information that you leave visible can still be read by recipients. Saving or exporting an edited result does not itself remove the original screenshot from your photo library.

6. Apple services and this policy website

Apple independently operates iOS, the App Store, and optional system services. Its handling of information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your settings. These services are separate from ChatBlur’s offline processing.

Opening an online copy of this policy or an external support page uses your browser or a system web viewer and requires an internet connection. The hosting provider, such as GitHub Pages, may process standard web request information, including IP addresses and browser information, under its own privacy policy. The policy pages supplied in this repository contain no analytics, advertising, cookies set by our code, remote fonts, or third-party scripts.

7. Information you voluntarily send for support

If you contact us outside the app, we receive only the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the issue, and retain it only as long as needed for those purposes or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion through the same contact channel, subject to applicable legal retention obligations. Your communication provider may also process the message under its own policy. Please do not send private screenshots or other sensitive content unless necessary; redact them first.

8. Children’s privacy

The app’s no-collection practices apply to all users, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us through a support channel, contact us so we can address it and delete it as appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We will publish any changes on this page and update the date above. If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update the policy and relevant App Store privacy disclosures and obtain any permission or consent required before the new processing begins.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent us, contact the ChatBlur developer at 1275966245@qq.com. We cannot access content that remains solely on your device.
